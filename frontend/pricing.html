Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an in-country test and a proxy test?

An in-country test starts a real Chrome browser in a data centre inside the country, so the load time is what a visitor there would see. For countries without a data centre, the browser runs in the nearest one and reaches your site through an address in the country. The page looks the same as it does to a local visitor, but the load time includes the extra hop, so we mark those results “via proxy”.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Sign in with Google and test from 4 countries, 5 times a day. You don't need a card.

What happens if I run out of tests?

New tests wait until your allowance resets at the start of your next billing month. On a paid plan you can add 25 proxy tests for $10 at any time, or move up to Team.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, at any time from your account. Moving up takes effect straight away; moving down or cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I pay?

By card through Stripe. Annual plans are paid once a year and cost 20% less than paying monthly.
